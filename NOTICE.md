# Notices and Disclosures

## Gridfinity

**Gridfinity** is a modular storage system created by
[Zack Freedman](https://www.youtube.com/c/ZackFreedman). It was first
published in the video
["Gridfinity: Your Ultimate Modular Workshop is FREE!"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ra_9zU-mnl8)
as an open framework for the community to extend, **released under the MIT
license**. Community information and the dimension standard are collected on
the unofficial Gridfinity wiki at [gridfinity.xyz](https://gridfinity.xyz/).

Gridfinity itself was partly inspired by Alexandre Chappel's
[Assortment System](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4160638), which is
licensed separately under CC-BY-NC-SA 4.0. This project does not use any
files or geometry from the Assortment System.

## How this project relates to Gridfinity

- **Independent project.** This generator is not made, endorsed, sponsored
  or maintained by Zack Freedman, Voidstar Lab, or the Gridfinity community
  wiki. "Gridfinity" is used only to describe compatibility with that
  system.
- **No Gridfinity files are included.** No code, CAD files or STL files
  from the original Gridfinity release, or from any other Gridfinity
  project, were copied. The script builds every baseplate from scratch in
  plain Python.
- **Dimensions only.** The script follows the published Gridfinity
  dimensions: a 42 mm grid, a 4.65 mm socket profile (2.15 mm chamfer,
  1.8 mm wall, 0.7 mm chamfer) and a 4 mm corner radius. Measurements are
  not copyrightable, and Gridfinity is MIT-licensed in any case.
- **Custom cell sizes are not Gridfinity.** Baseplates made with a `--cell`
  value other than 42 mm do not follow the Gridfinity standard and will not
  fit standard Gridfinity bins.
